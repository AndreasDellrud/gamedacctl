# Capture experiments

## Owner acceptance of the bounded approximation, 2026-11-09

After reviewing the native test outcomes and remaining gaps, the owner requested closure as “just close enough.” The sampled native behavior is accepted for task completion, including the subtle Pulse leading-hold difference; further controlled GG corroboration and definitive visual confirmation of that hold are waived as closure requirements. The repeat retained 46 packets over 273.170020 seconds: eight complete lighting features, 15 known output actions, and 23 zero-data completions. Filtering verified only bus 3/address 35, and privacy review found no USB descriptors, string/serial fields, or unrelated request families. The temporary whole-bus capture was deleted after successful filtering.

For these controlled inputs, three transition durations reproduce the entire report when computed by separately truncating the stored centisecond speed times each percent span. At 12.00 seconds, `1200 × [25,45,30] / 100` gives `[300,540,360]`. The independent research reconstruction in `src/capture.rs` reproduces all eight 1,024-byte feature reports byte-for-byte.
